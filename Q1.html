Tim Berners Lee

Tim Berners-Lee is an english computer scientist and the inventor of the World Wide Web. He helped create the first web browser and server. He was born in the UK back in 1955 and he graduated from the University of Oxford. With a degree in physics. Lee also invented HTML and HTTP along with URLs. A fun fact about Tim Berners Lee is that he was knighted in 2004 by Queen Elizabeth II for his contributions to the web.
